## Listing copy
**App name** (28/30): Paisa Flow: Cashbook & Split

**Short description** (77/80): Offline cashbook: multiple books, statement import, goals and bill splitting.

**Full description:**

Paisa Flow is a simple, private cashbook for everyday money. Track cash in and cash out across separate books — personal spending, your shop, a trip abroad — each in its own currency. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, works fully offline.

**Books that stay separate.** Create as many books as you need and see each balance at a glance. Filter entries by date, type, category or payment mode, with a running balance on every line.

**Import statements, skip the typing.** Bring in PDF bank statements or XLS wallet exports. Review every row before saving. Paisa Flow spots duplicates automatically, so importing the same statement again never doubles your entries.

**Split bills with friends.** Split one bill equally or by shares, or keep a group running for a trip or a shared flat. Settle up in a tap and it's recorded in your book.

**Goals that fill themselves.** Set a savings target, tag income to it, and watch progress update across all your books.

**Reports in seconds.** All entries, day-wise, category-wise or payment-mode reports. Export to PDF or Excel and share anywhere.

Light and dark themes · English and नेपाली · eSewa, Khalti, bank, card and cash payment modes.

**Category:** Finance · **Content rating:** Everyone · **Data safety:** no data collected · **Tags:** budget, expense tracker, khata
